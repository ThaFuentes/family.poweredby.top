Two keys. They do different jobs.

1 · Family key

Enter a household

Someone already in your family makes this. You type your name. The key does not need a family name — a note on their side is only so they remember who it is for. Starts with FAM-.

2 · Service key · vital

Get onto Family OS

Friends and family only. A Service key (or a trusted email) is how you get in the door at all. Stops random people from signing up. Starts with SRV-. It does not put you in someone else’s household — that’s the Family key.

Pantry and tools

Scan a UPC or a QR. Know what ran out, where the 10mm is, when the Civic needs oil.

Your name, your seat

You pick what you are called. A household label is optional — a note for whoever is handing out keys.

Paper trail

Citations, notices, tickets. Keep the copy, the city, the date. Go back to it if you need the file.
